# Revenue Rescue v1

## Fixed scope

**USD 350, one time, for one customer-authorized site and up to three listed,
twice-verified revenue-path defects.**

Supported defect classes are missing forms, dead calls to action, broken links,
DNS failures, raw shortcodes, booking failures, and contact failures. Initial
verification is ordinary read-only public navigation. It does not include
credential use, bypass attempts, stress testing, or a customer-system write.

More than three defects, more than one host, ambiguous ownership, or work
outside those classes requires a separate scope and quote.

## Included

- before-state evidence for the exact public path;
- a written fixed-scope repair plan;
- implementation in the existing site stack after authorization;
- a same-path after-state retest and evidence receipt;
- one same-scope correction pass if the listed acceptance test still fails.

## Required before implementation

1. The customer proves authority to approve the site change.
2. Both sides approve the exact defect list, exclusions, price, and acceptance
   tests in writing.
3. Cleared payment is independently recorded.
4. The customer grants least-privilege access.
5. A backup or rollback path is confirmed before mutation.

No credentials, customer records, payment data, or private evidence should be
placed in a public GitHub issue.

## Excluded and unclaimed

- outreach, hosting/domain transfer, redesign, SEO, and paid advertising;
- work on another host or on defects outside the approved packet;
- guaranteed leads, bookings, revenue, rankings, or regulatory compliance;
- causal attribution of later revenue to the repair without separate evidence.

A verified defect is evidence of broken conversion infrastructure. It is not
proof of how much revenue was lost or will be recovered.

## Acceptance boundary

Operational completion requires the authorized change, a repeat of the exact
public-path test, retained after-state evidence, and disclosure of any unresolved
item. A prepared packet is not a completed repair. An invoice or payment link is
not revenue; only cleared payment counts as paid work.

## Request a scope review

Email `am.statementforge@gmail.com` with only:

- the public site URL;
- the path that appears broken;
- what a normal visitor expected to happen;
- what happened instead.

Do not send passwords, account access, private customer data, or an unredacted
database in the first message. A file-transfer and retention plan must be agreed
before any non-public evidence is accepted.
